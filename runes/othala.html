Othala (ᛟ) is rune 24 of the 24 runes of the Elder Futhark, in Tyr's aett. Its name means heritage, estate.

Othala upright meaning

Heritage: home, family, property and what has come down to you. Roots and shared values give you a base to stand on.

Othala reversed meaning

Loss of home or roots. Inherited habits, prejudices or family quarrels hold you back. It may be time to let go of a tradition that no longer serves you.

Advice from Othala

Honor your roots without being bound by them.

Origin and history of Othala

Othala is heritage, meaning an estate or inheritance that passes down a family. It is related to the Germanic root behind words for noble, such as German edel. In the Elder Futhark the shape is a diamond on two legs, and in the later Anglo-Saxon alphabet it was called éðel. In the usual order it is the last of the twenty-four runes.

Othala in a rune reading

Othala asks what you have inherited and what you owe to it: home, family, property, tradition, or a place where you belong. In a reading it can mean roots, stability or a legacy. Reversed, it points to losing a home, family conflict, or a tradition that is holding you back.
