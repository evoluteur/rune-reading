Algiz (ᛉ) is rune 15 of the 24 runes of the Elder Futhark, in Heimdall's aett. Its name means elk, protection.

Algiz upright meaning

Protection and connection: a guardian, a safe boundary, an alert instinct. You are shielded as long as you stay awake to danger and to opportunity.

Algiz reversed meaning

Vulnerability or a hidden danger. A defense is down, or a warning sign is being ignored. Beware of a false sense of safety.

Advice from Algiz

Reach upward and stay alert.

Origin and history of Algiz

Algiz is usually read as elk, or in the Old English tradition as elk-sedge, a kind of reed. The sign resembles a hand with fingers spread, or an antler, and is often read as a gesture of warding. In the Younger Futhark the descendant of this shape took over the sound r, and it was then named for the yew.

Algiz in a rune reading

Algiz is protection and alertness: a guardian, a safe boundary, an instinct that senses danger, or a reminder that you are connected to something larger. In a reading it invites you to trust your defenses, and to ask what you are protecting. Reversed, it can mean vulnerability or a warning ignored.
