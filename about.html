Rune Reading

About runes

Runes are the letters of the early Germanic peoples. The word meant “secret” or “whisper”: the letters were used for writing, for carving names and charms on objects, and, in later tradition, for divination.

The Elder Futhark

The oldest runic alphabet has 24 runes and is named from its first six letters, F, U, Th, A, R and K. It is traditionally split into three groups of eight, the aettir, named for the first rune of each: Freyr’s, Hagal’s and Tyr’s. Every rune stands for a sound and also carries a word that names it:

Fehu
The sound F, and the word for cattle, and so for wealth.
Raidho
The sound R, and the word for riding, and so for a journey.
Othala
The sound O, and the word for inherited land, and so for home and heritage.

A short history

  • c. 98 CETacitus, in the Germania, describes Germanic peoples casting marked twigs to read omens. He does not mention runes.
  • c. 160 CESome of the earliest known runic inscriptions, such as the one on a comb found at Vimose in Denmark, date from about this time.
  • 5th centuryThe Kylver stone from Gotland carries the whole futhark in order.
  • c. 800In Scandinavia the alphabet shrinks to the 16-rune Younger Futhark, while the Anglo-Saxons grow theirs into the futhorc.
  • 13th centuryThe Poetic Edda, as recorded in Iceland, tells in the Hávamál how Odin hung nine nights on the world tree and took up the runes.
  • 1982Ralph Blum’s The Book of Runes popularizes rune casting in English. It adds a blank rune that has no ancient precedent.

What is known and what is revived

No ancient description of how runes were cast for divination survives. Modern rune reading is a revival, built on the rune names, on the medieval Old English, Norwegian and Icelandic rune poems, and on twentieth-century practice. That does not make it less useful as a way to reflect on a question, but it is worth knowing which parts are old.
